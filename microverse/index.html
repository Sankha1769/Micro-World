    MICROVERSE

    Explore the invisible universe

    MICROVERSE is a virtual microscope. More than fifty specimens, from a 100 nanometre virus to a hydra a centimetre long, are modelled in three dimensions and animated the way the living organisms move. Select any structure to read what it does.

    Rotate
    Drag with the mouse, or one finger on a touch screen.
    Pan
    Right-drag, Shift-drag, or two fingers.
    Zoom
    Mouse wheel, pinch, or the Zoom slider. The scale bar and magnification update as you go.
    Identify
    Click a structure. Click the same spot again to select the layer beneath it.
    Cross-section
    Slices the specimen at a chosen depth to expose its interior, like optical sectioning.
    Keys
    R reset view · Space pause · ← → previous / next specimen

    The models are simplified and scientifically informed. Sizes are typical values for each organism. Scale bars are calculated from the model's real dimensions, while the magnification readout uses the range a microscope would normally use for that kind of specimen. Some structures are exaggerated or rearranged so they can be seen at the same time.
